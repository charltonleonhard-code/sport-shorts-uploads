Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

1. Summary

Sport Shorts is a private tool with one user: its owner. It collects no data from anyone else, and it operates no server that stores personal information.

2. What the app stores

The app runs on its owner's own computer and stores the following there, and nowhere else:

  • Video files the owner adds to it, and the videos it produces.
  • Access tokens issued by YouTube, TikTok and Instagram when the owner signs in to those services. These are held in files readable only by the owner's user account, and are used solely to upload the owner's own videos.

3. Temporary public hosting

Instagram does not accept a direct file upload; it requires a publicly reachable web address and downloads the video itself. To allow this, a finished video may be placed briefly in a public repository so that Instagram can retrieve it, and is deleted immediately afterwards. Only videos the owner has chosen to publish are handled this way.

4. What is not collected

The app collects no analytics, sends no data to its author, uses no advertising or tracking, and shares nothing with third parties. It has no users other than its owner, and therefore holds no other person's data.

5. Data from platforms

When connected, the app reads only what it needs to publish: the account name it is posting as, and the status of uploads it has made. It does not read messages, contacts, or other people's content.

6. Withdrawing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the security settings of YouTube (Google Account), TikTok, or Instagram. Stored tokens can be deleted by removing the app's configuration files.
